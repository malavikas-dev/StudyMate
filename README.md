# 📚 StudyMate

> A smart and simple study management web application designed to help students organize their subjects, tasks, study sessions, and exam preparation.

## ✨ Features

- 📊 **Dashboard** – Overview of subjects, tasks, study hours, and progress
- 📚 **Subject Management** – Add, edit, and delete subjects
- ✅ **Task Management** – Create, complete, and delete study tasks
- ⏱️ **Study Timer** – Track focused study sessions
- 🍅 **Pomodoro Timer** – Study using focused Pomodoro sessions
- 📅 **Study Plan** – Calculate remaining study hours and daily study requirements
- 📈 **Analytics** – Track study hours and study activity
- 🕐 **Study History** – View previous study sessions
- ⚙️ **Settings** – Customize study preferences
- 🎨 **Modern UI** – Clean and responsive student-friendly interface
